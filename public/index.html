OUR STORY

THE DUBKL STORY

Bringing the authentic, laid-back pub culture of Australia and New Zealand directly to Changkat Bukit Bintang.

Founded by fans of southern hemisphere hospitality, Downunder Bar KL is more than just a place to grab a drink. It's a home away from home where expats, travelers, and locals assemble for major matches, premium tucker, and legendary conversations.

"Good Times. Great Drinks. Unmatched Company."
